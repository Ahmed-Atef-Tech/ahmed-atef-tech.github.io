About

I'm a hardware design engineer. Most of my work is taking a circuit from schematic to a finished, assembled board: choosing parts, laying out the PCB, designing for manufacturing and bringing the board up on the bench.

The boards rarely live on their own, so I also work on what's around them. I design sheet-metal and 3D-printed parts, build my own FDM printers and CNC machines, and put together pneumatic stations for simple production lines.

Work

Rev A, Rev B in progress

Isolated 4-channel AC controller

A KiCad board that lets a microcontroller switch four mains loads safely, with the logic side fully isolated from 220 V.

  • Four channels, 220 V at up to 8 A
  • Omron G3MB zero-cross solid-state relays
  • A fuse on every channel and a varistor on the mains input
  • LTV-814 optocoupler feedback, so firmware can confirm each output is live

Testing Rev A turned up issues I'm fixing in Rev B. The write-up will cover what went wrong and what changed.
