What I work on

From the circuit on the board to the machine around it. Hover an area to bring it into focus.

PCB design

KiCad, Altium

Taking a circuit from schematic to a board that a factory can make and assemble.

  • Schematic capture and part selection
  • Layout for 2-layer and 4-layer boards
  • Design for manufacturing and assembly
  • Bring-up and testing on the bench

Power and simulation

LTspice, SPICE

The power side of a board, checked in simulation before anything gets built.

  • Circuit simulation
  • Power stages and high-current paths
  • Isolated 220 V switching and protection
  • Motor driver and control circuits

Embedded systems

STM32, ESP32, Arduino

Microcontroller boards and the firmware that makes them do their job.

  • STM32 and ESP32 based boards
  • Arduino for quick prototypes
  • Firmware in C and C++

Sheet metal

SolidWorks

Enclosures and parts that hold the electronics in place.

  • Enclosures, brackets and mounting panels
  • Bends and tolerances a workshop can make
  • 3D models to check the fit with the PCB

3D printing and machines

FDM, CNC

Printing functional parts, and building my own printers and CNC machines.

  • Parts designed for FDM printing
  • Running and tuning FDM printers
  • Building 3D printers and CNC machines: frame, motion, wiring and setup

Pneumatics

Cylinders, valves

Pneumatic systems for simple production-line stations.

  • Pneumatic circuits with cylinders and valves
  • Sequencing the steps of a station
  • Electronic control of the pneumatics
