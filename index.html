NET: /ABOUT
About

      

        

          
I'm a hardware design engineer. Most of my work is taking a circuit from schematic to a finished, assembled board.

          
That means choosing parts, laying out the PCB, designing for manufacturing and bringing the board up on the bench. The boards rarely live on their own, so I also design what's around them: sheet-metal and 3D-printed parts, my own FDM printers and CNC machines, and pneumatic stations for simple production lines.

          
I also write small desktop tools when a task at my desk can be automated.

Isolated 4-channel AC controller

          
A KiCad board that lets a microcontroller switch four mains loads, with the logic side fully isolated from 220 V. Testing Rev A turned up issues I'm fixing in Rev B, and the write-up will cover what went wrong and what changed.

  • G3MB SSROmron zero-cross relays

  • Fuse + MOVper channel, surge on input
