PCB Design

Taking a circuit from schematic to a board that can be manufactured and assembled.

  • PCB layout for 2-layer and 4-layer boards
  • Design for manufacturing (DFM) and PCBA
  • Board bring-up and testing on the bench
KiCadAltium Designer
Power and Circuit Simulation

Checking circuits in simulation and designing the power side of a board.

  • Circuit simulation before building
  • Power stages and high-current paths
  • Isolated mains (220 V) switching and protection
  • Motor driver and control circuits
LTspiceSPICE
Embedded Systems

Microcontroller boards and the firmware that makes them do their job.

  • STM32 and ESP32 based boards
  • Arduino for quick prototypes
  • Firmware in C and C++
STM32ESP32ArduinoCC++
Mechanical Design and Sheet Metal

Designing the enclosures and parts that hold the electronics in place.

SolidWorksSheet Metal

FDM3D PrintingCNC
